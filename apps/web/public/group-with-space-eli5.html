Group With Space, Not Lines

How your brain sees order without prison bars.

Look at these 6 dots. You don't see 6 random dots. You instantly see 2 groups of 3. Notice: nobody drew a line between them!

3. The 3-Step Ladder of Grouping

When designing any interface, always try Step 1 first. Never jump straight to lines.

1 Whitespace Make gap between groups 2× bigger than gap inside. Zero visual noise.
2 Background Tint If space isn't enough, put one group inside a soft tinted card.
3 Divider Lines Last resort only. Use when content would otherwise bleed together.

Golden Rule for Beginners

"If you can tell they belong together without a line, delete the line."

Lines add visual weight. Whitespace adds breathing room. Great software feels calm because it uses space, not fences.
