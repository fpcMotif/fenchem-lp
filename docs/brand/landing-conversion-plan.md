# Fenchem landing — conversion plan

High-conversion audit of the production landing (variant-h) and the copy for
what's missing. Companion to `design-first-prompt-spec.md` (visual system) —
this doc covers structure, copy, and objection handling.

**One intent:** botanical-ingredient buyers (R&D formulators, sourcing
managers, QA leads) → **one action: request documentation/specifications**
(`sales@fenchem.com`, 24h response).

## 3. Benefits — reframed outcome-first

Same three pillars + two facts, rewritten benefit-first:

- **Qualify one vendor, cover three applications** — Nutrition, Food & Bev,
  and Personal Care actives from a single audited supply chain.
- **Documentation before sampling** — specifications, allergen statements,
  regulatory dossiers, and lot traceability arrive before you ask.
- **Potency you can put on a label** — standardized assays (≥95% curcuminoids,
  ≥98% CoQ10) validated by in-house chromatography on every lot.
- **Regulatory cover in 40+ markets** — dossiers prepared for your target
  jurisdictions, not generic paperwork.
- **Supply that survives disruption** — 30+ years, 6 global bases, documented
  chain of custody from field to finished extract.

## 4. How it works — buyer journey (3 steps)

1. **Send your target spec** — ingredient, assay, format, market. A technical
   response with matching specifications lands within 24 hours.
2. **Sample with full documentation** — CoA, allergen statement, and
   regulatory dossier travel with every sample; third-party verification on
   request.
3. **Scale with traceable supply** — production lots with chain-of-custody
   records, shipped from the nearest of six global bases.

## 5. FAQ (new section — place before the finale)

Answers grounded in repo facts; items marked **[confirm]** need a real number
from the company before ship — do not publish invented specifics.

1. **What documentation comes with a sample?** Specification sheet, CoA,
   allergen statement, and the regulatory dossier for your market — prepared
   before sampling, with lot traceability on request.
2. **Which certifications do your facilities hold?** ISO 9001, FSSC 22000,
   GMP, HACCP, Kosher, and Halal.
3. **Do you support our regulatory filings?** Yes — dossiers and
   documentation support for more than forty markets, prepared for your
   target jurisdiction.
4. **What are your minimum order quantities?** **[confirm exact MOQs]** —
   varies by ingredient and format; state the range honestly.
5. **How fast do you respond to a spec request?** Within 24 hours, from the
   regional office nearest you.
6. **Can you match a delivery format to our system?** Yes — powder, beadlet,
   oil suspension, and water-dispersible formats are selected around your
   delivery system.
7. **Where do you ship from?** Six bases: Nanjing (HQ/R&D), Hackensack,
   Frankfurt, Johannesburg, São Paulo, Kuala Lumpur.
8. **Can we audit your quality systems?** **[confirm audit policy]** —
   in-house labs validate identity, potency, and stability per lot;
   third-party verification available on request.
