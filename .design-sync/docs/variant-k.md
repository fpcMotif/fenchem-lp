Full-page landing variant — "Production · vivid" (Color Block): campaign-editorial color-blocking on VariantH's measured system. The current `DEFAULT_VARIANT` in the prototype registry.

Resolves the moodboard-vs-brand-book tension: division hues never become a primary surface — each division gets a SECTION WASH at 8–15% opacity (`bg-nutrition/15`, `bg-food/10`, `bg-cosmetics/10`) over a clean paper canvas, with the saturated color rationed to thick image frames, pill-chip borders, dots, and one oversized outlined numeral per band.

Type: Newsreader display + Plus Jakarta Sans body, set larger and friendlier than VariantH.

Measured color decisions (inherited from the 2026-08 WCAG pass):

- Primary CTA: `text-brand-green-950` on `bg-brand-green-500` (5.18:1); hover `bg-brand-green-400` (6.92:1). Never white on green-500.
- Blue is interactive-only (outline CTA, text links). Eyebrows/section numerals: `brand-green-700` on paper (5.73:1).
- Small-text floor `mute-600` (6.00:1); font-tech micro-labels floor 11px.
- Nutrition `#FFF67F` fails as text/stroke on paper at any size — its band strokes numerals in `brand-green-600` and reserves the yellow for washes and trims.
